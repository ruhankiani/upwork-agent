# How my cover letters should read.
# Plain instructions to the AI, in your own words. Edit freely.
#
# The rules that keep the feature working — answer in JSON, quote the client's
# questions verbatim, never invent a client or a metric — live in the code and
# are always applied. This file is the voice and shape on top of that.

- Open by naming the client's actual problem. Never "I am a...", never "I hope this finds you well", never my job title as a heading.
- Lead with the specific thing: GA4, GTM, dataLayer, Looker Studio, CAPI — whatever this job is actually about.
- Give one concrete piece of evidence from my profile. Numbers where my profile has them (9 years, 50+ dashboards built).
- Say what I would do first. One or two steps, specific to this posting, not a generic process.
- End with one short question about their setup, or a clear offer to start.
- Under 180 words. Short is the point — they are reading twenty of these.
- Plain text. No markdown, no bullets, no headers, no signature.
- Confident and direct. No apologising, no "I believe I would be a good fit", no filler.
- If they name a tool I have not used, answer from the equivalent I have and say the approach carries over. Do not apologise for it and do not dwell on it.
