Fetch from a link

      

        Optional shortcut. Give a public page — your Upwork profile, your site, an about
        page — and its text is dropped into the portfolio box below for you to keep or trim.
        It's copied as-is, nothing is rewritten. LinkedIn blocks this and will fail.
      

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      
Portfolio

      

        Type it, paste it, or paste your whole Upwork profile — formatting doesn't matter.
        It's stored exactly as you write it and the judge reads it directly when scoring.
        Worth adding in your own words: your rate, and any work you'd turn down.
      

      
      

        
        
      

    


    

      
Not interested in

      

        Work you'd turn down, in your own words — one per line, or a sentence, whatever.
        This is the strongest signal in the whole tool: anything matching here is treated
        as a deal-breaker rather than something the judge has to guess at.
        
Pay floors live in Preferences below, so you don't need to repeat them here.
      

      
      

        
        
      

    


    

      
Keywords

      

        Words that mean a job might be yours. A posting is skipped without being judged
        only if none of these appear in its title or skill tags — everything else goes to
        the judge, which decides properly against your portfolio and exclusions.
        
Read from your portfolio above, by pattern matching — no AI. Rebuild it after
        editing your portfolio, then prune anything odd. Keep it wide: a term here costs one AI
        call, a term missing means those jobs are never seen at all.
        One per line; # starts a comment.
      

      
      

        
        
        
      

    


    

      
Cover letter instructions

      

        How your letters should read, in your own words. Edited here, applied to every letter
        written from now on.
        
The rules that keep the feature working — answering in JSON, quoting the client's
        questions word-for-word, never inventing a client or a number — are in the code and always
        apply. This is the voice and shape on top of that.
      

      
      

        
        
      

    


    

      
Cover letter samples

      

        Letters that read the way you want yours to — real ones you have sent, or ones you have
        edited into shape. The AI copies the tone and structure, never the content: the
        facts always come from the job and your portfolio.
        
Separate each with a line of three dashes (---). Leave it empty to use
        the instructions above alone.
